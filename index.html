Day trips 🚗

Get out of the city for a day. Hire a car, or book a tour, and leave early.

🐧 Phillip Island · Evening

Penguin Parade

See the penguins and sit on the beach as they walk up to their holes. Can't make the trip? You can see the same thing in the city at St Kilda (pier/breakwater).

Great Ocean Road

About 1.5 hours from Melbourne to the start. Make it a full day trip: coastal drive, lookouts and beach towns.

🏄 Near Torquay · For surfers

Bells Beach

If he likes surfing: world famous. Many famous surf brands started around here, and it hosts a world surfing competition. It's on the way to the Great Ocean Road, so you can do both.

Pro tips 💡

How to do Melbourne like someone who lives here.

🍸

Ask the bartender

Ask any bartender for recommendations. Aussies love this. They'll often send you to the next great bar.

☕

Ask the barista too

New coffee places keep popping up. Ask baristas where they're drinking right now.

🥟

Juicy Bao table rules

Order the bao buns and the pork & chive dumplings, and use the soy sauce and black vinegar on the table.

🚶

Pair Degraves with Dukes

Degraves Street is nice to see but the food isn't great. It's near Dukes Coffee, so do both at once.

🍗

Get a parma at a pub

Chicken parmigiana at a pub is a Melbourne rite of passage. Try one at any of the pubs listed.

🌅

St Kilda evening

See the little penguins at St Kilda, then go to Chronicles Bar: one of the coolest bars, with great bartenders and vibe.

🥃

Try Australian whisky

Starward is Australian whisky and one of the best in the world. Whisky & Alement is the spot for a whisky bar.
